DiaryNote（每日日记）隐私权政策

更新及生效日期：2026年9月1日

开发者：liuwen-org

DiaryNote 是一款本地优先的日记应用。本政策说明应用如何处理日记内容、 应用设置及其他相关数据。

一、应用处理的数据

应用会在你的设备本地处理你主动输入的日记正文、日期、天气、心情、 所选图片以及应用设置。这些数据仅用于创建、显示、搜索、编辑和导出日记。

应用不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、身份证号、精确位置、 通讯录、通话记录、短信、相机或麦克风数据。

二、数据收集和网络访问

应用不申请互联网访问权限，不包含广告或统计分析 SDK，不会将日记内容、 图片、应用密码或应用设置上传至开发者服务器。

开发者不会通过本应用收集用户数据，也不会出售用户数据或将用户数据分享给其他公司或组织。

三、图片、导出和分享

应用仅通过 Android 系统图片选择器访问你主动选择的图片，不会扫描未选择的相册内容。 当你主动导出或分享日记时，相关内容会发送到你选择的文件位置或第三方应用。 后续处理受相应接收方的隐私政策约束。

四、系统备份

根据你的 Android 设备设置，操作系统或设备厂商提供的备份和设备迁移功能 可能会备份应用数据。此类处理由操作系统或备份服务提供商负责，不会将数据提供给 DiaryNote 开发者。你可以在设备的系统设置中管理或关闭备份功能。

五、数据保存和删除

日记和应用设置通常保存在设备本地，直到你在应用中删除日记、清除应用数据 或卸载应用。导出的 PDF、图片和其他文件由你自行管理和删除。

六、安全措施

应用使用 Android 应用隔离、本地数据库和可选应用密码降低未经授权访问的风险。 请妥善保管设备、系统解锁凭据、应用密码和导出文件。

七、未成年人保护

应用不要求用户提供年龄或身份信息。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用， 并避免在日记或导出文件中记录或传播不必要的敏感个人信息。

八、政策更新

如果应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将相应更新。 请在应用更新后查看本页面的生效日期和内容。

九、联系我们

如对本隐私权政策有疑问、投诉或请求，请发送邮件至： YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

DiaryNote Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: September 1, 2026

Developer: liuwen-org

DiaryNote is a local-first diary application. This policy explains how the app handles diary content, application settings, and related data.

1. Data handled by the app

The app processes diary text, dates, weather, moods, selected images, and application settings locally on your device. This data is used only to create, display, search, edit, and export diaries.

The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, call logs, messages, camera data, or microphone data.

2. Data collection and network access

The app does not request Internet access and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Diary content, images, the app password, and application settings are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

The developer does not collect user data through the app and does not sell or share user data with other companies or organizations.

3. Images, exports, and sharing

The app accesses only images you explicitly select through the Android system photo picker. When you export or share a diary, the selected content is sent to the file location or third-party application you choose. Further processing is governed by that recipient's privacy policy.

4. System backups

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system or device manufacturer's backup and device-transfer services may back up application data. These services are controlled by the operating system or backup provider and do not provide the data to the DiaryNote developer. You can manage or disable backups in your device settings.

5. Data retention and deletion

Diaries and settings remain locally on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You control and delete exported PDFs, images, and other files separately.

6. Security

The app uses Android application isolation, a local database, and an optional app password to reduce unauthorized access. You should protect your device, system credentials, app password, and exported files.

7. Children

The app does not request age or identity information. Minors should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian and should avoid recording or distributing unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the application's features or data-handling practices change. Please review the effective date and content after updates.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, complaints, or requests, contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
